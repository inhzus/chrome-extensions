# V2EX Inn Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-19_

V2EX Inn is an unofficial browser extension for v2ex.com. It is not affiliated with V2EX.

## What data the extension handles
- **Tags you create:** the usernames you tag and the tags you give them.
- **Settings:** your preferences, such as how many hot replies to show.
- **Check-in status:** the date of your last daily check-in, so it runs only once a day.

## Where it is stored
All of this data is stored **only in your own browser**, using `chrome.storage.local`. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself with the "Export JSON" button.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.
- The extension contains no analytics, ads or trackers, and never contacts any third-party server.
- Its only network requests are to v2ex.com itself: when auto check-in is on, it opens `v2ex.com/mission/daily` with your existing V2EX session, at most once a day, to claim the daily login reward. You can turn this off in the options page.
- It runs only on v2ex.com pages. There it reads page content (usernames and reply like counts) to show your tags and the hot-replies panel. That content is processed locally and is not stored or sent anywhere.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual tags from the extension's toolbar popup, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.

## Changes
Any changes to this policy will be published in this file.

## Contact
Questions or issues: https://github.com/inhzus/chrome-extensions/issues

# V2EX Inn 隐私政策

V2EX Inn 是非官方的 v2ex.com 浏览器扩展，与 V2EX 官方无关。

- 扩展只保存你自己添加的用户标签、设置和上次签到日期，全部存储在你本地的浏览器中（`chrome.storage.local`），除非你自己使用“导出 JSON”，否则数据不会离开你的设备。
- 不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人或使用数据；没有统计、广告或追踪，不连接任何第三方服务器。
- 唯一的网络请求发往 v2ex.com 本身：开启自动签到时，每天最多一次使用你现有的 V2EX 登录状态访问 `v2ex.com/mission/daily` 领取每日登录奖励，可在设置中关闭。
- 仅在 v2ex.com 页面运行，读取用户名和回复的感谢数以显示标签和热门回复，这些内容只在本地处理。
- 可在工具栏弹窗中删除标签，或卸载扩展以清除全部数据。
- 问题反馈：https://github.com/inhzus/chrome-extensions/issues
